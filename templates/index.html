About Mysuru

Mysuru (Mysore) is a city in the southern Indian state of Karnataka. Known for its heritage structures, palaces, and vibrant culture, Mysuru is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India.

This platform helps tourists and locals discover the best services, attractions, restaurants, hotels, and everything else the beautiful city has to offer.
